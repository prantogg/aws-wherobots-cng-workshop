Read before quoting.
· yearbuilt is construction year, not roof age.
· Hail is radar-detected density, not modelled severity.
· Tornado / wind / winter are tract-resolution, not per-building.
· Hazard is centroid-sampled for 98.24% of buildings.
· Assessor $ values are popup-only reference (≈63% coverage, county-dependent); never used for styling or ranking.
· NULL road/station distance = furthest (no station within 25 km), not missing — on a building, and on a parcel that has buildings. A parcel with no buildings reads “no building to measure”: nothing was measured there, which is not the same claim as remoteness.
· Access drive time is a modelled estimate in 5-minute bands — straight-line distance × a terrain factor calibrated against HERE routes, not a per-building route.
· Aerial imagery is locational context only, including at partial opacity — blending it under the footprints changes what you can SEE, never what was measured. Scores are screening indicators from modelled and tract-resolution inputs — never derived from imagery. A building shown under aerial is not individually assessed.

Assessor $ coverage — why not dollars

Improvement-value coverage is ~63% and county-dependent. The largest exposures often publish the least — El Paso (the state's biggest, 290,630 buildings) reports $0. A dollar ramp maps who reports, not where risk is.
